InMyEar

Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

InMyEar is designed to help users reflect on conversations they choose to provide. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use InMyEar and how that information is handled.

Information you provide

When you use InMyEar, you may choose to provide:

  • Text that you type or paste into the app.
  • Screenshots that you select for conversation analysis.
  • Information contained within those conversations or screenshots.

You should only submit information that you are comfortable providing for analysis.

How information is used

Information you submit is used to generate the analysis and reply suggestions requested through the app.

InMyEar does not use submitted conversations or screenshots for advertising or sell them to third parties.

AI processing

InMyEar uses OpenAI's API to process submitted text and screenshots and generate analysis results.

OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default unless an API customer explicitly opts in to data sharing.

Backend services

InMyEar uses Supabase to provide backend infrastructure, including Edge Functions and database services used to operate the app.

InMyEar does not intentionally store the contents of your submitted conversations or screenshots in its application database after an analysis request is completed.

Rate limiting and security information

To protect the service from abuse, InMyEar may process a network identifier associated with a request. The app stores a cryptographic hash derived from that identifier together with a timestamp for rate-limiting purposes rather than storing the raw identifier in the rate-limit database.

Old rate-limit records are periodically removed.

Data retention by service providers

Third-party service providers may retain information for limited periods in accordance with their own security, abuse-prevention, legal, and data-retention policies.

OpenAI currently states that standard API data may be retained for a limited period for abuse monitoring and operational purposes, subject to its applicable policies.

Security

InMyEar uses reasonable technical safeguards intended to protect data transmitted through the service. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-party content

Conversations and screenshots may contain information about other people. You are responsible for deciding whether it is appropriate to submit that information to InMyEar for analysis.

Children's privacy

InMyEar is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit conversation text or screenshots. You can also remove selected screenshots before requesting an analysis.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as InMyEar changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact
